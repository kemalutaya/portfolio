About

Administrative support, not clinical care.

I'm Kem Alutaya, a Medical Virtual Administrative Assistant based in the Philippines with 5+ years of healthcare-adjacent experience, supporting healthcare workflows remotely for teams across the U.S., UK, and Australia.

My experience is primarily focused on the operational side of healthcare: keeping patient information organized, verifying insurance eligibility, maintaining accurate records, preparing charts, reviewing documentation, and helping healthcare teams stay on top of their daily administrative workload.

In my most recent role with an eye care organization, I worked extensively with Compulink and PointClickCare while handling insurance eligibility across multiple states and payers. I regularly reviewed patient records, prepared charts for upcoming physician schedules, performed facility and patient-status verification, processed EOBs, and supported facility communications.

Before moving into medical virtual assistance, I worked in healthcare operations at Optum, including prior authorization support, backend escalations, documentation, workflow support, and agent coaching.

My approach is simple: be accurate, be dependable, understand the workflow, and make the team's job easier.

Case 03

Automating Administrative Draft Generation

Problem
Sending physician schedules required checking patient count, status, and insurance across every building a doctor was visiting that day (often 5+ buildings per doctor) before the schedule could go out.
Before → After
Check patient count, per building Check status, per building Check insurance, per building Manually draft & send, 5+ buildings Draft pre-filled with facility, date, and files, down to a few clicks to review and send
My Contribution
Designed and built this myself: a lightweight tool that collapses the multi-step manual process into a few clicks. I still review and send it myself each time.
Result
Presented the workflow and it was approved for operational use.
~2 hrs saved per doctor, per day on schedule prep alone
Estimated, based on the previous manual schedule-prep process.
