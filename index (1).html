Process Improvement

I look for the repetitive task worth fixing.

Case Study Highest Impact

Cutting a Three-Hour Daily Task to One

3 hrs → 1 hr Daily preparation task, before and afterEstimated, based on the previous manual process.
Problem
Three recurring daily tasks were fully manual, repetitive, and error-prone in predictable ways: wrong recipient, wrong date, wrong attachment, typos. The kind of task most admin roles have at least one of.
What I did
Mapped the manual process, identified which parts were genuinely repeatable versus which needed a human decision, and built the repeatable part into Excel, Outlook, and Word macros using tools already available on the machine, since installing anything new needed IT approval.
The judgment call
I automated the mechanical part and kept a human review step before anything went out. The goal was to spend my attention on the part that actually needed it, not to remove myself from the process.
Result
The task dropped from about three hours a day to about one. The workflow was presented and approved for ongoing use.
